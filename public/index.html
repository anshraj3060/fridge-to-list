        The honest part

        A camera sees what is in the fridge, not what is gone. So this list is an inference: staples you told us you keep, minus the ones visible in your photos, plus anything the model thinks looks nearly empty. A 4-billion-parameter model is small and genuinely can misread a jar of pickles as relish. Check the "what the model saw" panel before you trust the list.

        No photos are uploaded anywhere. They are resized in your browser, sent to the local Ollama server on this machine, and dropped from memory when the request ends.
